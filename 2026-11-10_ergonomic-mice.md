# The Best Ergonomic Mice for Preventing Strain in 2024

**Quick Summary:**
* **Best for Vertical Grip:** Logitech MX Vertical
* **Best for Trackball:** Logitech MX Ergo
* **Best for Standard Grip:** Logitech MX Master 3S

Repetitive strain from traditional mice can lead to discomfort and long-term injury. Ergonomic mice are designed to place your hand in a more natural position, reducing tension in your wrist and forearm. The right choice depends on your preferred grip style and the precision required for your work.

**Logitech MX Vertical: The Natural Position**
The MX Vertical is designed to place your hand in a "handshake" position, which significantly reduces forearm strain. It features a high-quality sensor and customizable buttons, making it a top choice for those who suffer from wrist fatigue and want a distinct change from traditional mouse designs.
